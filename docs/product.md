# Product Vision

Nabe is a self-hosted DNS control plane for personal devices, family networks, homelabs, and small trusted groups.

Nabe is not a NextDNS clone. It is designed for people who want to own their DNS infrastructure and still have a product-grade control plane for devices, ownership, policies, and visibility.

Nabe is not an AdGuard fork. AdGuard Home is the first DNS Engine that Nabe manages through an adapter. AdGuard resolves DNS; Nabe manages users, Device Clients, Client Tokens, DNS instances, permissions, policies, log visibility, and auditability.

The initial product starts with one admin user, Kyle, and one Cloud DNS master backed by AdGuard Home. The long-term shape includes family and friends, multiple users, self-service device management, and Edge Nodes connected by the Speiche Agent.
